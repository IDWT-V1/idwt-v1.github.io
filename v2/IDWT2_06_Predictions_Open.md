# Infinite Dimensional Wave Theory, Second Edition — Part 6: Predictions and Open Problems

## 1. How this theory can die

A framework that derives its numbers from two integers and a reference mass has nowhere to hide. Its structural claims are rigid — no parameter can be moved to absorb a contrary observation — so it is falsifiable in the strong, single-observation sense. This Part collects the ways it can be killed, the measurements it is waiting on, and an honest ledger of what it has not yet derived.

One distinction runs through everything: a *residual* is a small, structurally understood gap inside measurement uncertainty; a *falsifier* is a claim with no adjustment available — either the geometry is right or the framework is wrong.

## 2. Single observations that would falsify the framework

**Neutrinoless double beta decay — any confirmed signal.** The neutrino's five-dimensional sector cannot support the spinor structure a Majorana mass requires, at any order, forever (Parts 1, 3). This is the sharpest prediction in the framework: experiments now running (nEXO, LEGEND-1000, KamLAND-Zen) will push sensitivity through the next decade, and every improvement is a survived test — or the end. There is likewise no see-saw and no lepton-number violation of any kind; discovery of any Majorana-type operator falls under the same axe.

**Inverted neutrino mass ordering.** The neutrino indices are fixed and the count function only grows, so the ordering is necessarily normal. A definitive inverted-ordering measurement (JUNO, DUNE, Hyper-K) is fatal. Current data prefer normal at $3$–$4\sigma$.

**Any new fundamental particle.** The spectrum is complete at fifteen. There is no sixteenth mode, no fourth generation, no sterile neutrino, no supersymmetric partners, no WIMPs, no axions as fundamental modes. The capacity reading of the electroweak sector sharpens this: the four heaviest particles exactly saturate a fixed budget, leaving headroom only below roughly $20$ GeV where no admissible mode exists — so a new fundamental particle at *any* accessible mass falsifies the closure. Every post-top collider null result to date is this prediction passing.

**A fourth light neutrino species.** The invisible Z width must count exactly three. It does: $2.984 \pm 0.008$.

**Failure of exact CKM unitarity.** The mixing matrix is unitary by construction (Part 3). If the first-row deficit currently discussed as the Cabibbo Angle Anomaly settles as genuine non-unitary physics, the counting mechanism is dead.

**A confirmed glueball.** There is no propagating colour field to bind; a state established as pure glue, incompatible with any quark assignment, falsifies the colour sector.

**Right-handed weak couplings.** The weak interaction's left-handedness is Kähler geometry; genuine right-handed W couplings beyond known radiative effects would break it.

**Tree-level $\rho \neq 1$.** W and Z share one sector; their mass relation is fixed by two integers.

## 4. The open problems

The ledger, in decreasing order of weight. Nothing here is hidden in a footnote; this is what a reader should weigh against Parts 1–5.

**1. Mode selection — the central open problem.** Stated in Part 1, restated here as the headline: the framework does not yet derive from the equation of motion why the fifteen occupied standing waves are the ones that fire. What exists: exact exclusion of the odd levels (a parity theorem), positive radiative width driving every even non-member to decay (derived), a closed generative rule that reproduces the spectrum exactly with no false positives, and the capacity-saturation account of why the spectrum *terminates*. What does not exist: a dynamical derivation that the generative rule itself is forced. Every route tried — energetics, combinatorial minimality, characteristic classes — has returned a clean negative, which is itself informative: the selection is spectral, not energetic. This is where the theory's remaining evidential weight funnels: if the selection is derived, the framework becomes an essentially parameter-free account of the particle spectrum; until then, it is a closed rule that agrees with nature at six sigma against chance.

**2. The one quantum.** The size of a single excitation — the quantum of action — is provably not derivable from the framework's own axioms (a scaling argument shows no classical observable can fix it). It enters as one import: a stable excitation carries unit charge. The number is one, nothing is fitted, and everything discrete — detection clicks, absolute decay rates — discharges through it. But it is an import, and the framework says so. Relative rates and all mass/angle predictions are independent of it.

**3. The equidistribution premise.** The counting law behind the mixing matrix (and several hadron results) rests on a proved mechanism — equal weight per state, exact on every sector geometry — whose *applicability* premise (that a pointlike excitation loads its tower equally) traces to the particle-size program and is not yet independently derived.

**4. Absolute decay rates.** The rate *formalism* is native and complete — the muon lifetime comes out end-to-end within a fraction of a percent — but absolute rates in the scalar channel carry the one-quantum normalization (item 2). Rate ratios are parameter-free.

**5. The cosmological constant.** The unoccupied dimensions contribute exactly zero, which confines the problem but does not solve it: the occupied sectors' vacuum energy has no derived suppression, and the observed smallness is unexplained. A first-order gap, owned as such.

**6. Dark matter.** The complete spectrum offers no candidate; the framework accounts for none of the astrophysical evidence. What *is* derived is a constraint: dark matter cannot be higher-dimensional gravity leaking into our three (a uniform-source theorem — ordinary matter feels zero gradient from anything gravitating only in hidden dimensions). Whatever dark matter is, here it would have to be mass that gravitates through the shared three dimensions while staying electromagnetically dark; no such object's abundance or stability is derived.

**7. The strength of gravity.** $G_\infty$ is a second dimensional input (Part 4). Reducing two inputs to one is a program, not a result.

**8. Named smaller items.** The bottom quark's beat mechanism (site forced by three exact coincidences; the two-mode beat dynamics not derived). The electron's join at $13$ (exact; dynamically unforced). The Higgs closure at the up-type sum. The deeper operator origin of the third neutrino's $1/35$. The solar-splitting $1.6\sigma$ tension. The Weinberg-angle $+0.37\%$ structural residual. Confinement's linear law at mechanism level. Spin-1 polarization structure of the bosons from the spinor field. Each is tracked, none is load-bearing for the Parts that precede this one.

## 5. The shape of the situation

Step back and the picture is unusual for a fundamental proposal. On one side: every measured fundamental mass to sub-percent accuracy from two integers and one unit; the mixing angles as counts; the couplings cascading from the same seeds; the quantum catalogue and the tetrahedral angle derived rather than postulated; gravity's structure with the equivalence principle as a theorem; a falsifier list an experimentalist can act on this decade, several entries of which are being tested right now. On the other side: one central derivation missing (why these modes), one import (the quantum), two cosmological absences (the constant, dark matter), and a handful of named residuals.

The framework's wager is explicit. It does not claim completeness; it claims that what is derived is derived honestly, that what is open is stated plainly, and that no observation now in hand contradicts the picture of one wave on an infinite-dimensional manifold. The experiments that can settle the wager are running.
