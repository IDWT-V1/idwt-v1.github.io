## 1. Single observations that would falsify the framework

Each entry states the prediction *and the mechanism that makes it rigid* — the reason no parameter can save the theory if the observation goes the other way.

**Any confirmed neutrinoless double beta decay.** The mechanism (Part 3 §3.3): spinor algebra in five dimensions — the neutrino's home — admits no charge-conjugation structure, so no lepton-number-violating term can be *written*, at any order. Not suppressed: unwritable. Every tightening of the experimental bound (KamLAND-Zen now; nEXO and LEGEND-1000 pushing toward few-meV sensitivity through the 2030s) is a survived test, and one confirmed signal ends the framework. This is simultaneously the no-see-saw and no-Majorana statement: discovery of any Majorana mass term or lepton-number violation falls under the same axe.

**A definitively inverted neutrino mass ordering.** The three neutrino levels are $10 < 15 < 22$ and the counting function only grows, so $m_{\nu_1} < m_{\nu_2} < m_{\nu_3}$ *necessarily* — an inverted hierarchy cannot be written. JUNO, DUNE, and Hyper-Kamiokande will settle the ordering; current data prefer normal at $3$–$4\sigma$.

**Any new fundamental particle, at any mass.** The sector list is derived and terminates (Part 1 §3); within the sectors the spectrum stands complete at fifteen. No fourth generation, no sterile neutrino, no supersymmetric partners, no new fundamental state anywhere: one confirmed discovery reopens what cannot be reopened without abandoning the sector derivation. Every null result since the top quark — three decades of them — is this prediction passing. Two specific absences worth naming: nothing at $68.3$ GeV (the empty $d=10$ level 35, inside LEP's range and duly absent) and nothing tau-like below $51.7$ TeV.

**A fourth light neutrino species.** The invisible Z width counts neutrino species and must read exactly three. Measured: $2.984\pm0.008$.

**Cleanly established non-unitarity of the CKM first row.** Mixing weights here are intensity fractions of one wave and must sum to one (Part 3 §7). The current "Cabibbo angle anomaly" — beta-decay $|V_{ud}|$ versus kaon $|V_{us}|$ missing unitarity by $\sim3\sigma$ — is a tension between two measurements, and the framework (which matches the kaon value at $0.1\sigma$) stakes itself on unitarity holding as it settles.

**A confirmed glueball.** No colour field exists to bind (Part 3 §2); a state established as pure glue, with quantum numbers no quark assignment can carry, falsifies the colour sector.

**Genuine right-handed weak couplings.** The weak interaction touches only the odd-degree (holomorphic) spinor components — a property of the complex geometry, not a choice (Part 3 §3.2).

**Tree-level $\rho \neq 1$.** W and Z inhabit one sector; their mass relation is two integers, $\bigl(S(76,2)/S(81,2)\bigr)$, with nothing to adjust.

**A measured deviation from $1/r^2$ gravity, at any scale, or gravitational missing energy at a collider.** Part 4's projection theorem permits neither; there is no compactification radius below which anything changes and no graviton to escape.

## 3. Candidate results, premises named

Two results carry physical mechanisms the framework has built but not finished; they are stated here as candidates so the reader can weigh them, not oversold as settled.

**The quark CP phase, $\gamma = 67.5°$.** Mechanism: among all quark transitions, charge selection permits a complex phase to survive at exactly one cross-sector hop ($u$-sector to $d$-sector, the $V_{ub}$ element); the phase accumulated there is a winding — a rate fixed by the complex geometry's class count (three) times a cycle fraction fixed by the resonance site (one-sixteenth) — giving $3\times360°/16 = 67.5°$ against the measured $\sim65.7°$. The open premise: the identification of the cycle step. **The $|V_{ub}|$ magnitude** follows from the same counting law as the other mixing elements applied to that one hop: $3.7\times10^{-3}$ ($-0.6\sigma$), completing a Jarlskog invariant within $1\sigma$ — with the pairing of counts motivated, not forced. Both live one derivation short of the standard the rest of this edition holds; both are falsifiable now at the $3\sigma$ level by the quantities they touch.

## 4. The open ledger

In decreasing order of weight, mysteries stated as mysteries.

**1. Which levels are occupied.** The framework's central unknown, flagged in every Part that touches it. The sector list is derived; given the fifteen observed levels, everything downstream performs at the fraction-of-a-percent level; the *emptiness* between the particles has real mechanics (odd levels parity-blocked from the vacuum, even non-members radiatively unstable — nothing else can persist); and the occupied levels display the striking arithmetic web of Part 2 §7. But no derivation selects the fifteen. This edition's position, held deliberately: the levels are measured facts, the arithmetic relationships are findings, and we do not build facts out of numbers we do not understand. A future derivation would convert the mass spectrum from a six-sigma-beyond-chance description into a closed prediction; until then the mystery stands, labeled.

**2. The quantum of action.** One import, provably underivable from the framework's own axioms (the scaling proof, Part 5 §1): a stable excitation carries unit charge. Everything absolute-rate and every click discharges through it; everything dimensionless is independent of it. Open in the strong sense that a completion dynamics — *why* a transition finishes as a unit — would replace the import with a mechanism.

**3. The cosmological constant.** The empty dimensions contribute exactly zero (derived); the occupied sectors' condensates carry real vacuum energy with no derived suppression. The observed smallness is unexplained — a first-order gap, owned.

**4. Dark matter.** No candidate in the fifteen; the astrophysical evidence unaccounted for. What is derived is a constraint with teeth (Part 4 §4): dark matter cannot be higher-dimensional gravity leaking into our three — that channel is closed by theorem — so whatever it is must be genuine mass pulling through the shared three dimensions while staying electromagnetically dark.

**5. The strength of gravity.** $G_\infty$ is the second and last dimensional input. Deriving it — reducing the framework to one scale — is a program with no current result. (And no hierarchy problem waits inside it: the kernel couplings and the manifold stiffness are different kinds of quantity that never form the infamous ratio.)

The equidistribution premise beneath the counting law (mechanism proved as geometry; physical applicability resting on the particle-size result). The bottom beat's dynamics (site exact, geometric mean argued dimensionally, full mechanism open). The lone-pair share $\delta$ in bent molecules (fitted, not derived). The Z's $+0.05\%$ and the Weinberg angle's $+0.37\%$ — real structural residuals, unexcused. Confinement's fringe-amplitude coefficient. Absolute rates beyond the one-quantum import.
