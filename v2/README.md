# Infinite Dimensional Wave Theory — Second Edition

A restatement of IDWT organized around a single question asked of every claim:
*is this a physical phenomenon that could actually be happening?* Everything
here passed that test; derivations, status accounting, and numerical
verification remain in the first-edition Parts 1–11 and `files/idwt.py`.

The second edition carries no status symbols. What is included is the physics;
what is open is stated in prose where it stands.
